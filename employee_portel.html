Regulatory Framework Overview: FDA & EMA

Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) regulations govern the methods, equipment, facilities, and controls used in pharmaceutical manufacturing. Both the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and the European Medicines Agency (EMA) maintain comprehensive frameworks that manufacturers must comply with to ensure product quality, safety, and efficacy.

21 CFR Part 211 — The FDA's Current Good Manufacturing Practice regulations for finished pharmaceuticals establish minimum requirements for methods, facilities, and controls used in the manufacturing, processing, packing, or holding of a drug product.

The EMA's equivalent framework, the EU GMP Guidelines (EudraLex Volume 4), covers similar principles but includes additional requirements specific to the European market. Understanding both frameworks is essential for pharmaceutical professionals operating in global markets.

Key areas covered by both regulatory frameworks include: personnel qualifications, premises and equipment validation, documentation requirements, production controls, quality control testing, distribution practices, and complaint handling procedures.
