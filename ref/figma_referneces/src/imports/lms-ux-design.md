The pharmaceutical Learning Management System (LMS) is a mission-critical, event-driven ecosystem. To maintain compliance with 21 CFR Part 11 and GxP standards, the User Experience (UX) must prioritize data integrity (ALCOA+), traceability, and "validation-ready" transparency. Below is the UX design for each role across the critical event workflows.🔁 1. SOP Update → Retraining WorkflowSME / Trainer: Receives a "Revision Required" task on their dashboard. The UI provides a side-by-side comparison of the old SOP vs. the new draft. Quality Assurance (QA): Views a "Sign-off" queue. The UX includes a mandatory "Meaning of Signature" dropdown (e.g., "Approval of Training Content") and a password re-authentication prompt. Employee: Their dashboard instantly updates with a high-priority "Retraining Required" alert. The SOP document is presented in a "locked" viewer that prevents printing or saving until the mandatory reading time has elapsed. System: Automatically archives the previous version and logs the event in the audit trail with an $NTP-synchronized$ timestamp. 👨‍💼 2. New Employee Onboarding WorkflowTraining Administrator: Accesses an "HRIS Sync" dashboard showing new hire records. A "Rule-Based Assignment" wizard confirms the curricula assigned based on the role (e.g., "Filling Operator"). Employee: A guided "Onboarding Journey" view appears. Progress bars indicate "Day 1 Essentials" (Safety/GMP) vs. "Role-Specific Skills." System: Background sync ensures that the employee’s unique User ID is created and mapped to the correct "Security Role" without manual entry. 🧪 3. Deviation Event → CAPA WorkflowQuality Assurance (QA): In the QMS-integrated view, the user selects a "Deviation ID" and clicks "Assign Training." They can choose specific employees or entire shifts. Employee: Receives a notification with the context of the deviation. The training module includes a mandatory "Reflection" or "Root Cause" assessment to ensure the $Score \ge 80\%$ reflects true comprehension. System: Automatically updates the CAPA record in the QMS once the training is signed by both the employee and the supervisor. 🧾 4. Audit Preparation / 10. Inspection EvidenceQuality Assurance (QA): Activates "Inspection Mode." This UX presents a simplified, read-only interface with advanced filters for "Batch Number," "Date Range," and "SOP Version." Auditor (External): Interacts with a secure "Auditor Portal." They can click any record to see a "Signature Manifestation" (Printed Name, Date/Time, and Intent). Training Administrator: Uses a "Bulk Export" feature to generate a validated PDF "Audit Packet" containing the complete history of a specific manufacturing line. ⏳ 5. Certification Expiry WorkflowEmployee: Dashboard features a "Compliance Health" widget. Certificates nearing expiry (within 30 days) turn yellow; lapsed ones turn red. System: Sends automated "Escalation Alerts" to the employee's manager if a certification is not renewed within 48 hours of expiry. System / Automation: Re-calculates "Qualified Status" in real-time, potentially triggering a "Hard Lockout" in the MES (Manufacturing Execution System). 📚 6. New Training Course ReleaseSME: Uses an "Assessment Builder" with randomized question pools to prevent answer-sharing. Quality Assurance (QA): Reviews the course in a "Staging Environment" before clicking "Release to Production." Employee: Receives a "New Opportunity" notification. The UX allows "Pausable Learning," where they can stop at Slide X and return later without losing progress. 🔄 7. Department Transfer WorkflowTraining Administrator: Updates the "Job Role" attribute. The UX shows a "Curriculum Gap Analysis" highlighting which trainings are "Transferred" (Equivalency) vs. "New Requirements." Employee: Their dashboard clears archived tasks and populates new "Gap Training" modules. A "Transition Map" shows their progress toward full qualification in the new role. 📉 8. Compliance Drop Alert WorkflowTraining Administrator: Views a color-coded "Risk Heatmap." If a department's compliance falls below $95\%$, the cell turns red. System: Triggers a "Notification Burst" to all non-compliant employees in the affected department. Quality Assurance (QA): Receives an "Executive Summary" report via email, allowing them to intervene before a formal audit finding. 🧾 9. Training Completion WorkflowEmployee: Upon passing a quiz, the UX triggers an "Electronic Signature Modal." The user must re-enter their unique credentials. System: Generates a "Dynamic Certificate" with a unique QR code for verification. The record is permanently locked in the "Immutable Audit Trail." Supervisor: Receives a "Verification Task" on their mobile device for On-the-Job Training (OJT) sign-offs, using a digital checklist. 🧑‍💻 System / Automation Background UXLog View: For IT admins, a "System Health" log shows real-time $NTP-synchronization$ status and API handshake successes with QMS/HRIS systems. Security UX: Enforces "Session Timeouts" after 15 minutes of inactivity and "Account Lockout" after 3 failed login attempts, displaying clear instructions for password recovery via a verified process.
